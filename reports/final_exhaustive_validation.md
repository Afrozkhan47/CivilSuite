2. **Mass Balance / Fresh Density:** Fresh concrete density $\rho_{\text{concrete}} \equiv \text{Cement} + \text{Water}_{\text{batch}} + \text{FA}_{\text{batch}} + \text{CA}_{\text{batch}} + \text{Admixture}_{\text{batch}}$ (delta $< 1.0\text{ kg/m³}$).
3. **Water-Cement Ratio:** Structural W/C ratio $W/C \equiv \text{Design Water} / \text{Cement}$.
4. **SSD Mix Ratio:** Structural mix ratio $\text{Cement} : \text{FA}_{\text{SSD}} : \text{CA}_{\text{SSD}}$ uses **SSD Design aggregate quantities** without moisture corruption.
5. **Moisture Balance:** Batch water accounts for free surface moisture and absorption per IS 10262:2019 Clause 7 without altering structural dry/SSD mix proportions.
6. **Non-Negativity:** All component masses and volumes remain non-negative.
7. **Numerical Integrity:** Zero occurrences of `NaN`, `Infinity`, or unhandled float overflows.
